Three user portals over one shared design system, built along the Phase 0–4 roadmap. Architecture from OpenUp, cultural intimacy from Transiti.

What we're building

Four surfaces, one design system

Every screen draws from a single component library and multilingual content layer. Three of the surfaces are user-facing portals; the fourth is the internal back office that runs the moat — matching, vetting, and content.

Portal · B2C

Member Portal

Where members onboard in their mother tongue, track their journey, book facilitators, and attend sessions. The heart of the product.

Model: OpenUp's private-account → self-assessment → personalised recommendations → check-ins flow, wrapped in Transiti's warm, mother-tongue session intimacy.

Portal · Supply

Facilitator Portal

Where culturally-matched facilitators manage their profile, availability, session space, and member context — the supply side of the moat.

Model: Transiti's branded, secure session space, extended with lightweight facilitator ops (calendar, notes, group tooling).

Portal · B2B

Employer / HR Portal

Where employer buyers see anonymised, aggregate wellbeing and engagement insights, manage seats, and activate their workforce. The revenue engine.

Model: OpenUp's business-EAP dashboards and wellbeing-ROI framing — never individual data, only aggregates.

Foundation · Internal

Design System & Ops

Tokens and components from the brand guidelines, an i18n/RTL content layer, plus the internal back office for facilitator vetting, matching, and the content CMS.

Model: Own it. This is the layer the references don't show — and where Tamia4Life's differentiation lives.

The build, phase by phase

Sequencing the surfaces

Each phase advances the surfaces in step with the venture roadmap. Nothing gets built before it's needed, and the multilingual layer is structural from the first commit — never retrofitted.

Phase 0

Foundation & Validation

1–2 months

Aim: prove the surfaces before building them — and pour the foundation everything else stands on.

Design system & ops

  • Design tokens from the brand guidelines — colour, type, spacing, the sprout mark.
  • Decide the i18n / RTL architecture and multilingual content model (day-one decision).
  • Component primitives: buttons, inputs, cards, the language selector.

Member portal

  • Clickable prototype of the three priority screens — no backend.
  • Onboarding step 1: mother tongue + cultural context selector (the signature).
  • Usability-test the prototype in members' own languages.
ReferenceDocument OpenUp's onboarding/self-assessment flow and Transiti's secure session space as concrete patterns before writing code. Exit gate: validated screens + a working prototype.
Phase 1

MVP — Member Portal core

3–4 months

Aim: a working member portal delivering real sessions in 1–2 launch languages, with the facilitator side just deep enough to supply it.

Member portal

  • The three screens live: onboarding, “My Journey” dashboard, facilitator match & booking.
  • Live 1:1 session space (booking + video) — Transiti-style branded secure room.
  • Starter self-guided content library + first resilience/productivity check-in.
  • B2C freemium + paid tier via Stripe.

Facilitator portal v1

  • Profile, availability calendar, and the session space.
  • Minimal member context + post-session notes.
ReferenceOpenUp's flow — account → assessment → personalised recommendations → check-ins — becomes the member spine; Transiti's cultural warmth sets the session tone. Harden the component library from the prototype into production parts.
Phase 2

Employer (B2B) Portal

3–4 months

Aim: stand up the revenue engine — the employer portal — and widen member language coverage.

Employer / HR portal

  • Anonymised, aggregate wellbeing & engagement dashboard.
  • Seat management, employer onboarding + activation materials, SSO.
  • Per-seat billing.

Member & facilitator

  • Expand to 3–5 languages; add group programs / masterclasses surfaces.
  • Facilitator portal gains group-session tooling.
  • Matching engine strengthened with real usage data.
ReferenceOpenUp's business-EAP dashboards and ROI framing, adapted almost verbatim — but the privacy-as-trust guardrail is absolute: employers see aggregates, never a single member's data.
Phase 3

Scale & Prove Impact

4–6 months

Aim: take the portals mobile and instrument them to prove ROI.

Reach

  • Native mobile apps at member-portal parity.
  • Broader language coverage + a growing facilitator network.
  • Content personalisation engine and expanded library production.

Proof

  • Formal outcome measurement — wellbeing + productivity/retention.
  • Deeper analytics dashboards across member, facilitator, employer.
  • Workflow automation for facilitator ops.
ReferenceThis is where OpenUp's wellbeing-ROI story stops being a pitch and becomes real data you can put in front of investors and enterprise buyers.
Phase 4

Institutional & Expansion

Ongoing

Aim: harden the surfaces for institutional partners and expand into new communities.

Institutional surfaces

  • Public / municipal partner surfaces or white-label access.
  • Accessibility & compliance hardening to EU public-sector standards.

Depth at scale

  • Localisation at scale; new sectors, regions, communities.
  • Back-office maturity: matching quality, facilitator supply, measured impact.

Guardrails on the references

What not to copy

The references are patterns to learn from, not identities to inherit. Three things to leave behind on every surface.

OpenUp's visual identity

Reads corporate-Northern-European. Tamia4Life needs a warmer, multicultural, human language that signals belonging.

Transiti's clinical framing

Therapy-first and academic. Keep Tamia4Life preventive, modern, and accessible — strength before struggle.

Anything crisis-centred

Both references occasionally lean clinical. Stay on the growth-and-resilience side of the line, with clean referral paths out.
