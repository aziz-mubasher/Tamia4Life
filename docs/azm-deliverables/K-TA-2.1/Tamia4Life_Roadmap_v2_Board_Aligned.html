Venture Roadmap · v2 · Board-aligned

Tamia4Life

From surviving to growing — one phase at a time.

Preventive, non-clinical emotional wellness for immigrants and international professionals in Italy, delivered in their mother tongue, scaling across the EU.

Supersedes Roadmap v1. v1 used a self-invented Phase 0–4 scheme that did not exist on the AZM Startup board. This version conforms to the canonical Phase 1–6. Content and sequencing are unchanged in substance — only the phase structure and labels are corrected. See the mapping table below to translate any v1 reference.

The roadmap climbs the arc the product promises its members — seed → sprout → sapling → young tree → flowering → grove. Language waves are marked per phase.
Phase 1
1–2 months

Discovery & Problem Validation

Seed

Goal: prove the problem is real, costly and unserved — before designing anything.

  • Customer discovery interviews across three tracks: end users, employer buyers, facilitators (S TA 1.1).
  • Test the load-bearing assumptions — recurring strain, language as the barrier, a named and costed employer pain with a budget owner.
  • Facilitator sourcing pilot in the Wave 1 language communities — prove they can be recruited and vetted.
  • Confirm the space between “I'm fine” and “I need a psychiatrist” is real to members.
Exit GateDemand validated · at least a few employers with a costed pain and a letter of intent · a plausible facilitator supply signal.
Phase 2
1–2 months

MVP Definition & Design

Sprout

Goal: decide exactly what to build, and prove the design works before code.

Wave 1 · Arabic + Romanian
  • Community & Language Strategy — ten target communities, language/script mapping, EU scope (S TA 2.B).
  • Build-vs-configure decisions and the tech stack; GDPR groundwork and EU-hosted infrastructure.
  • Design system with multilingual script coverage from day one, never retrofitted.
  • Clickable prototype of the three priority screens, usability-tested in members' own languages.
  • Matching engine v1 spec and the MVP build backlog.
Exit GateValidated screens, a locked stack, and a sized backlog the team can build from.
Phase 3
3–4 months

MVP Build

Sapling

Goal: a working product that delivers real sessions.

Wave 1 live
  • The three priority screens: language/culture onboarding, “My Journey”, facilitator match & booking.
  • Session space — rented video SDK inside a branded, warm, private room.
  • Facilitator Portal v1 — profile, availability, session access, private notes.
  • Starter content library, resilience & productivity check-in, Stripe freemium + paid.
  • Configure the commodity, build the moat: matching, the check-in index, the in-language journey.
Exit GateA member can onboard in their mother tongue, get matched with a reason, pay for and complete a real session, and check in.
Phase 4
2–3 months

Private Beta & Validation

Young tree

Goal: run it for real, with real members, before opening the doors.

  • Launch with the pilot employer or a single language community — chosen for workforce overlap with Wave 1.
  • First paying members; measure completion, repeat bookings and early retention.
  • Tune the matching weights against real usage — the config-not-code decision pays off here.
  • Harden privacy, accessibility and RTL parity; prove the clinical referral path works.
Exit GateA working service with real retention data and a repeatable facilitator supply.
Phase 5
3–4 months

Public Launch

Flowering

Goal: switch on the revenue engine — the employer layer — and open generally.

Wave 2 · Bengali + Urdu + Punjabi
  • Employer / HR portal with anonymised, aggregate wellbeing insights — never individual data.
  • Per-seat billing, employer onboarding and activation, SSO.
  • Wave 2 languages live — the South Asian bloc, three new scripts including Nastaliq.
  • Group programs and masterclasses; public go-to-market.
Exit GateTwo to three paying employer contracts and a repeatable B2B onboarding motion.
Phase 6
Ongoing

Growth & Iterate

Grove

Goal: scale reach, prove impact, and become durable infrastructure.

Wave 3 · Ukrainian, Albanian, Tagalog, Hindi
  • Native mobile apps; broader language and facilitator coverage.
  • Formal outcome measurement — wellbeing plus productivity and retention — to prove employer ROI.
  • EU expansion: Spain, Germany, Poland/Czechia, France/Benelux. Language assets travel; nothing hard-codes Italy.
  • Public, municipal and EU-funded integration partnerships — revenue and validation.
  • Continuous iteration on the moat: matching quality, facilitator supply, measured impact.
HorizonA durable, credible institution — not a product that plateaus.

Guardrails — true in every phase

Mother tongue first

The signature moment. Never let it slip into a settings menu.

Preventive, not clinical

Growth and resilience language, with clean referral paths when clinical care is needed.

Productivity as the throughline

Every feature ties back to focus, energy, balance and performance.

Privacy as trust

Visible confidentiality is a feature, not fine print. Employers see aggregates only.
