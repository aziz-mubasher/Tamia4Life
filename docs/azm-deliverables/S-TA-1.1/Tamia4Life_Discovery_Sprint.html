The sampling rule that can silently fail the sprint

A2 will validate by construction if the sample is drawn badly. Interview only Punjabi, Urdu, Arabic and Bengali speakers and A2 validates — meaninglessly. Brescia’s two largest foreign communities are Romanian (24,711, rank 1) and Albanian (~16,000, rank 2). The sample must span the language-distance gradient. If A2 holds only at the high-distance end, that is a scoping result: it names the launch languages.

The scorecard refuses VALIDATED on A2 while the low and low–mid rows are empty.

  • Sample foreign-origin, not only foreign citizens. Brescia granted 9,716 naturalisations in 2024. Recruiting only through sportelli immigrazione misses them and the second generation.
  • Gender skews by route. Domestic and care work is 85% non-Italian and heavily female (Centro Migranti). The Senegalese population is ~65% male.
