# Assumption scorecard — S TA 1.1

Aggregate validation across customer discovery waves. Update after each synthesis session (every ~5 interviews per segment).

**Statuses:** `validated` · `mixed` · `invalidated` · `pending`

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **validated** | Multiple interviews show consistent past behaviour and concrete evidence without prompting |
| **mixed** | Some supporting evidence, material contradictions, or only hypothetical interest |
| **invalidated** | Repeated evidence that the assumption does not hold |
| **pending** | Not enough data yet |
