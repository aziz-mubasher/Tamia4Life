**Rule from CLAUDE.md §6.3, applied to discovery:** no claim may be made from evidence this file

---

## 0. How to fill this

- Score after each **wave** (~5 interviews per segment), not after each interview.
- Three states only: **VALIDATED** · **CONTRADICTED** · **INSUFFICIENT**. There is no "leaning yes".
- Every state change must cite at least two interview IDs and carry at least one **verbatim quote**.
- Record **evidence against** as carefully as evidence for. A row with only supporting evidence is a
  row nobody tried to disconfirm.
- `INSUFFICIENT` at the end of the sprint is an honest and useful answer. `VALIDATED` on thin
  evidence is not.

**Interview ID format:** `TA-{segment}-{nn}` — e.g. `TA-A-07` (resident), `TA-B-03` (employer),
`TA-C-02` (facilitator), `TA-D-01` (institution).

## 6. Data handling — applies to this file

- **Pseudonymous IDs only.** No names, no employers' identifying details in the quote fields.
- **Never recorded anywhere in this repo:** immigration or legal status, religion, ethnicity, health
  information, or free text that invites any of them (CLAUDE.md §7).
- **Bands, not exact values,** for age, years in Italy and employment status.
- Notes and recordings live on EU-hosted systems, pseudonymised, deletable on request. Recording only
  with explicit, separate consent.
- Interpreters and mediators handling personal data need **written appointments as authorised persons
  under art. 29 GDPR** — an NDA is not sufficient.
