# Customer Discovery Interview Guide

**Task:** S TA 1.1 — Customer Discovery Interviews (15–20 users)  
**Phase:** 1 · Discovery & Problem Validation  
**Venture:** Tamia4Life — a mother-tongue, culturally-matched emotional-wellness / adaptation service for multicultural communities in Italy.

## Objective

Validate the problem before building. Run customer-development interviews (grounded in [The Mom Test](https://momtestbook.com/) and Steve Blank's method) across three parallel tracks — end users, employer buyers, and facilitators — to test load-bearing assumptions **A1–A7**.

This is a **research/validation task**, not an application feature. Talk about their life, not your idea. Listen for past behaviour, concrete costs, and unprompted pain — not compliments or hypotheticals.

---

## Riskiest assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Segment | Priority this round |
|----|------------|---------|---------------------|
| **A1** | Real recurring emotional strain | End user | High |
| **A2** | Language/culture is the barrier to getting help | End user | **Sharpest** |
| **A3** | The strain is costly and already acted on | End user | High |
| **A4** | Owned, quantified employer people-pain | Employer | **Sharpest** |
| **A5** | A real wellbeing/inclusion budget + named buyer + buying process | Employer | High |
| **A6** | Culturally-matched facilitators recruitable at viable rates | Facilitator | Light pass |
| **A7** | A legitimate space between "I'm fine" and "I need a psychiatrist" | All | High |

**Focus:** demand (A1–A5, A7) with a lighter supply pass on A6. Weight interviews and listening toward **A2** and **A4**.

---

## Interview targets

Run in waves of ~5 per segment. Synthesise between waves. Stop when interviews stop surprising.

| Segment | Target range | Wave size |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| End users | 15–25 | ~5 |
| Employer buyers | 8–12 | ~5 |
| Facilitators | 5–8 | ~5 |
| Institutions (optional) | 3–5 | ~3 |

---

## Ethical guardrails (non-negotiable)

- Interview in the participant's **mother tongue** where possible.
- This is **not therapy** — do not diagnose or probe trauma.
- If someone becomes distressed, respond warmly and **signpost** appropriate support (e.g. local helplines, GP, community services).
- **Never** collect immigration or legal status.
- Consent is **informed, voluntary, and revocable** — participants may skip any question or stop at any time.

---

## Screeners

Use screeners to qualify participants before scheduling. Adapt wording to mother tongue; keep criteria consistent.

### End user screener

Must meet **all**:

1. Lives in Italy and identifies with a non-Italian cultural / linguistic background (or is part of a multicultural household).
2. Has experienced emotional or adaptation strain related to living/working in Italy in the **last 12 months** (self-reported; no diagnosis required).
3. Age 18+ and able to consent independently.
4. Willing to discuss past experiences and what they have **already tried** (not hypothetical interest in a new service).

Disqualify if: recruited primarily as a friend of the team without fitting the profile; currently in acute crisis requiring immediate clinical intervention (signpost instead).

### Employer buyer screener

Must meet **all**:

1. Works at an organisation with **50+ employees in Italy** (or actively manages people programmes for such an org).
2. Has visibility into **HR, wellbeing, inclusion, or people operations** budgets or decisions (not just an individual manager with no programme authority).
3. Has dealt with **multicultural workforce** challenges in the last 24 months (retention, integration, absenteeism, engagement, etc.).
4. Can speak to **specific past initiatives** and what they cost — not only future plans.

Disqualify if: no authority or influence on wellbeing/inclusion spend; vendor-only contact with no internal buyer access.

### Facilitator screener

Must meet **all**:

1. Professional background in counselling, psychology, social work, coaching, community mediation, or comparable facilitation (licensed or experienced).
2. Fluent in **Italian plus at least one other language** relevant to target communities.
3. Has delivered support, coaching, or group work to **migrants, expats, or multicultural groups** (paid or volunteer).
4. Willing to discuss **availability, rates, and constraints** honestly.

Disqualify if: no direct client-facing experience; unable to discuss commercial terms.

### Institution screener (optional)

1. Represents a municipality, NGO, health authority, or community organisation serving multicultural populations in Italy.
2. Has run or funded programmes related to integration, mental health, or community wellbeing.
3. Can describe **funding sources and decision processes** for such programmes.

## Interview scripts

**General rules (Mom Test):**

- Ask about **specific past events**, not opinions about your solution.
- Avoid pitching Tamia4Life until the last 5 minutes (optional "concept probe" only).
- When they say "I would use that," ask what they **did last time** they felt that way.
- Dig into **cost**: time, money, relationships, work performance, sleep, missed days.

Copy [`templates/interview-notes-template.md`](templates/interview-notes-template.md) for each session.

---

### End user script (~45 min)

**Opening (5 min)**

- Thank them; confirm consent, recording (if any), and right to skip/stop.
- "I'm learning how people experience life in Italy — not selling anything. There are no right answers."

**Past behaviour — strain (A1, A7)**

1. Tell me about a time in the last year when adapting to life in Italy felt emotionally heavy. What was going on?
2. How often does something like that come up — weekly, monthly, rarely?
3. When it happens, what do you usually do first? Walk me through the last time.
4. Did you ever feel you needed more support than friends/family could give, but less than a psychiatrist? What happened?

**Barriers (A2 — weight heavily)**

5. Have you looked for professional or structured support? What did you try?
6. What got in the way — language, culture, cost, trust, stigma, time, something else? Which mattered most?
7. If support existed in your language with someone who understands your cultural context, would that have changed what you did? **What did you actually do instead?**

**Cost and action (A3)**

8. What did this strain cost you — work, relationships, health, money? Be specific.
9. What have you already spent time or money on (apps, community, private sessions, travel home, etc.)?
10. What would have made you switch approach?

**Concept probe (optional, last 5 min)**

- Brief neutral description: mother-tongue, culturally-matched emotional wellness between everyday stress and clinical care.
- "Does this match a problem you've already tried to solve? How does it compare to what you did?"

**Close**

- Anything we should have asked? May we follow up once?
- Signpost support if needed. Thank them.

---

### Employer buyer script (~45 min)

**Opening (5 min)**

- Confirm consent; frame as learning about how organisations support multicultural teams — not a sales call.

**People pain (A4 — weight heavily)**

1. What people-related challenges have been most costly for your organisation with multicultural employees in the last 12–24 months?
2. Tell me about a **specific incident or pattern** — absenteeism, turnover, conflict, disengagement, EAP under-use?
3. Who owned that problem internally? What did they measure?
4. What did it cost — turnover, recruitment, productivity, grievances, brand? Numbers or estimates welcome.

**Budget and buying (A5)**

5. What programmes or benefits exist today for employee wellbeing or inclusion? What do they cost per year?
6. Who **signs off** on new wellbeing/inclusion spend? Walk me through the last time something new was approved.
7. Have you piloted anything targeted at language/cultural barriers to support? What happened?
8. If a pilot cost €X per employee per year, what threshold would need to be met to try it?

**Gap (A7)**

9. Do employees fall between "fine" and clinical mental health services? How do you see that gap today?
10. What happens when someone doesn't fit EAP or traditional counselling?

**Supply sanity check (A6, light)**

11. Have you worked with bilingual or culturally matched facilitators? What worked or didn't?

**Concept probe (optional, last 5 min)**

- Neutral one-paragraph description of Tamia4Life for employers.
- "Does this map to a problem you're already paying to address? What would you compare it to?"

**Close**

- Follow-up permission; thank them.

---

### Facilitator script (~40 min)

**Opening (5 min)**

- Consent; learning about how practitioners serve multicultural clients in Italy — not recruitment yet.

**Experience and fit (A6, A7)**

1. Describe your work with migrants, expats, or multicultural clients in Italy. Typical presenting issues?
2. How often do clients say language or cultural mismatch blocked them from other services?
3. Where do you see the gap between "everyday adaptation stress" and psychiatric care?

**Supply and economics (A6)**

4. What languages and cultural communities do you serve? Where are you based?
5. What are your typical rates, formats (1:1, group), and weekly capacity?
6. What would make you **decline** a steady stream of employer-referred clients?
7. What onboarding, supervision, or licensing constraints apply?

**Demand signal (A2, A7)**

8. What do clients usually try before they reach you? What still fails?
9. If an employer paid for culturally matched sessions in the client's mother tongue, how would that change access?

**Concept probe (optional, last 5 min)**

- Short neutral description of Tamia4Life facilitator role.
- "Is this something you've already been doing informally? What would need to be true for you to commit 10 hrs/month?"

**Close**

- Referrals to other facilitators? Follow-up permission; thank them.

## Exit gate for S TA 1.1

Move to the next phase when **all** of the following hold:

| Criterion | Required state |
|-----------|----------------|
| A1–A3 | **Validated** with end users — recurring strain, real barriers (especially A2), costly and already acted on |
| A4–A5 | At least a **few employers** with named, costed pain, a budget owner, and real pilot interest |
| A6 | **Plausible supply signal** (light pass is sufficient) |
| A7 | Supported across segments — clear middle ground between "fine" and psychiatry |

**Decision rules:**

- Demand validates but **no buyer/budget** appears → iterate the model; **do not start building**.
- Pain does **not recur unprompted** → pause and reframe the problem.
- Interviews still surprising → run another wave before deciding.

---

## Suggested workflow

1. **Recruit** using screeners; schedule in participant mother tongue.
2. **Interview** using the segment script; capture notes in the template.
3. **Synthesise** after each wave (~5 interviews): update scorecard, list surprises, adjust next wave questions.
4. **Decide** at exit gate; document decision in scorecard "Exit decision" section.
