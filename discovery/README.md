# S TA 1.1 — Customer Discovery Interview Guide

**Venture:** Tamia4Life — mother-tongue, culturally-matched emotional-wellness / adaptation support for multicultural communities in Italy.

**Phase:** 1 · Discovery & Problem Validation  
**Method:** Customer development grounded in [The Mom Test](https://momtestbook.com/) and Steve Blank's customer discovery.

This guide supports parallel interview tracks across **end users**, **employer buyers**, and **facilitators**. Run in waves of ~5 per segment, synthesise between waves, and stop when interviews stop surprising you.

## Riskiest assumptions (A1–A7)

| ID | Assumption | Primary segment | This round |
|----|------------|-----------------|------------|
| **A1** | Real **recurring** emotional strain (not one-off stress) | End user | **Heavy** |
| **A2** | **Language / culture** is the barrier to getting help | End user | **Sharpest knife — weight heavily** |
| **A3** | The strain is **costly** and they have **already acted** on it | End user | **Heavy** |
| **A4** | **Owned, quantified** employer people-pain | Employer | **Sharpest knife — weight heavily** |
| **A5** | Real wellbeing/inclusion **budget + named buyer + buying process** | Employer | **Heavy** |
| **A6** | Culturally-matched facilitators **recruitable at viable rates** | Facilitator | Lighter pass |
| **A7** | Legitimate space between *"I'm fine"* and *"I need a psychiatrist"* | All | **Heavy** |

**Focus:** Demand (A1–A5, A7) with a lighter supply pass on A6.

## Ethical guardrails (non-negotiable)

- Conduct interviews in the participant's **mother tongue** where possible.
- This is **not therapy** — do not diagnose, label, or probe trauma.
- If someone becomes distressed, **pause**, acknowledge, and **warmly signpost** appropriate support (e.g. local helplines, GP, employee assistance).
- **Never** collect immigration status, legal status, or documentation details.
- Consent is **informed, voluntary, and revocable** at any time.
- Do not record without explicit permission; prefer notes over audio when in doubt.

## Consent & logistics

**Before the interview (2 min):**

> "I'm researching how people from multicultural backgrounds in Italy experience emotional wellbeing and access to support — not selling anything today. There are no right answers. You can skip any question or stop at any time. I won't ask about immigration or legal status. May I take notes? [If recording:] May I record — you can ask me to delete it anytime."

**Log each session:** date, segment, language used, screener pass/fail, consent (notes / audio / none).

**Duration:** 30–45 min end user & facilitator; 45–60 min employer.

## Screeners by segment

Use these before booking a full interview. A "pass" means the person fits the learning goal — not that they are a customer yet.

### End user screener

| # | Question | Pass if… |
|---|----------|----------|
| EU1 | Are you living in Italy and did you grow up speaking a language other than Italian at home? | Yes |
| EU2 | In the last 12 months, have you felt ongoing stress, loneliness, or emotional difficulty — not just a bad week? | Yes (signals A1) |
| EU3 | Have you looked for support (formal or informal) for how you were feeling? | Either — we learn from both paths (A3) |
| EU4 | Are you currently in acute crisis or primarily seeking clinical treatment today? | **No** — signpost to appropriate care instead |
| EU5 | Comfortable speaking for ~30 min about your experiences (not trauma details)? | Yes |

**Disqualify:** acute crisis (refer out), under 18 without guardian consent, unwilling to skip immigration/legal topics if they arise.

### Employer buyer screener

| # | Question | Pass if… |
|---|----------|----------|
| EB1 | Do you influence or own budget/decisions for employee wellbeing, inclusion, HR, or people operations? | Yes — note title & scope |
| EB2 | Does your organisation employ a multicultural workforce (non-Italian-born or multilingual)? | Yes |
| EB3 | In the last 24 months, have people-related issues (retention, absenteeism, engagement, inclusion) been discussed at leadership level? | Yes (signals A4) |
| EB4 | Is there any line item or approved spend for wellbeing, EAP, or inclusion initiatives? | Either — critical for A5 |
| EB5 | Can you speak to specific incidents or metrics, not just general concern? | Yes |

**Disqualify:** no connection to people/budget decisions; purely vendor sales call with no problem exploration.

### Facilitator screener

| # | Question | Pass if… |
|---|----------|----------|
| F1 | Do you provide counselling, coaching, community support, or wellbeing facilitation? | Yes |
| F2 | Are you fluent in Italian **and** at least one community language (e.g. Arabic, Bengali, Romanian, Chinese, Spanish)? | Yes |
| F3 | Have you worked with immigrant or multicultural clients in Italy? | Yes |
| F4 | Would you consider short-format, culturally grounded emotional support (not clinical psychiatry)? | Open answer — explore A7 & A6 |
| F5 | What is your typical hourly rate or session fee today? | Willing to share range (A6) |

### Institution screener (optional)

| # | Question | Pass if… |
|---|----------|----------|
| I1 | Role at municipality, health authority, NGO, or community organisation serving immigrants? | Yes |
| I2 | Visibility into unmet wellbeing / adaptation needs in your catchment? | Yes |
| I3 | Existing referral pathways or gaps for non-Italian-language support? | Either |

## Interview scripts

**Principles (Mom Test):**

- Ask about **past behaviour**, not hypotheticals ("Would you use…?").
- Ask about **their** life, not your idea.
- Listen for **emotion, cost, and workarounds** — unprompted recurrence validates A1.
- Follow the thread when A2 or A4 surface; don't lead the witness.

---

### End user script (~30–45 min)

**Opening (5 min)**  
Consent, language preference, "tell me a bit about your life in Italy."

**Past behaviour — strain (A1, A3)**  
1. "Think back over the last year — when did you feel most emotionally drained or isolated? What was going on?"  
2. "How often does something like that come back — weekly, monthly, seasonally?"  
3. "What did that cost you — sleep, work, relationships, money, health?"  
4. "What did you **do** about it? Walk me through the last time you tried to feel better."  
5. "What worked even a little? What didn't?"

**Barriers (A2 — sharpen here)**  
6. "When you looked for help — or thought about it — what got in the way?"  
7. "Was language part of it? Culture — feeling understood, shame, privacy, family expectations?"  
8. "Have you ever had support in your mother tongue here? What was different?"  
9. "If Italian-only services were perfect in every other way, would you still want something culturally matched? Why?"

**Solution space (A7)**  
10. "What's 'too much' help — when would you say 'I need a psychiatrist' vs 'I'm fine'?"  
11. "Is there something in between you've wished existed? Have you tried anything in that space?"  
12. "Have you paid for informal support — community leader, coach, online, back home by phone?"

**Wrap (5 min)**  
13. "Who else do you know with similar struggles — how do they cope?"  
14. "Anything I should have asked?"  
Thank them; signpost if needed.

---

### Employer buyer script (~45–60 min)

**Opening (5 min)**  
Role, scope, team size, multicultural makeup.

**Owned pain (A4 — sharpen here)**  
1. "When did people issues last hit leadership's agenda — retention, absenteeism, engagement, inclusion? What triggered it?"  
2. "Put a number on it if you can — turnover cost, sick days, engagement scores, complaints."  
3. "Who **owns** that problem internally? Who gets blamed if it doesn't improve?"  
4. "What have you already tried in the last 24 months? Budget spent? Vendors? Internal programmes?"  
5. "What failed — and why?"

**Budget & process (A5)**  
6. "Is there a wellbeing, EAP, or inclusion budget line today? Rough size and renewal cycle?"  
7. "Walk me through the last time you bought something in this space — who signed off, how long, criteria?"  
8. "If a pilot cost €X per employee per year, what threshold needs a board sign-off vs your discretion?"  
9. "Have multicultural or language-specific wellbeing needs come up explicitly? From whom?"

**Solution framing (A7, light A2)**  
10. "Do employees surface language or cultural barriers to using existing EAP/wellbeing?"  
11. "Is there appetite for something between 'awareness campaigns' and clinical referral?"  
12. "Would you pilot with a subset — which sites/teams and why?"

**Wrap**  
13. "If this problem disappeared tomorrow, what would you reallocate budget to?"  
14. Referrals to other HR/wellbeing leads.

---

### Facilitator script (~30–45 min)

**Opening**  
Background, languages, communities served, current practice model.

**Supply & fit (A6, lighter)**  
1. "Describe your typical client in Italy — origin, language, presenting needs."  
2. "What do clients struggle to find in mainstream Italian services?"  
3. "Your rate structure today — session length, fee, cancellations, no-shows?"  
4. "Would you take culturally matched, short-format emotional support sessions at [probe range]? What would make it viable?"  
5. "Recruitment — do you know others who would join? What would they require?"

**Middle space (A7)**  
6. "Where do you draw the line between support you offer and psychiatric/clinical care?"  
7. "What situations do you refer out — and to whom?"  
8. "Have clients said they wanted something 'lighter' than therapy but 'more' than talking to friends?"

**Wrap**  
9. "Biggest operational headache in serving multicultural clients in Italy?"  
10. Willingness to stay in touch for a future pilot pool (optional).

## Note capture & synthesis

**Per interview:** copy [`templates/interview-notes.md`](templates/interview-notes.md) into `notes/` (create the folder when you begin fieldwork) and name it:

```
notes/YYYY-MM-DD_<segment>_<anonymous-id>.md
```

Example: `notes/2026-07-21_end-user_EU-03.md`

**Between waves (~5 interviews):**

1. Read all notes in the wave; highlight **exact quotes** tied to A1–A7.
2. Update [`assumption-scorecard.md`](assumption-scorecard.md) — do not score on gut feel alone.
3. Note surprises and adjust the next wave's probes (especially A2 & A4).
4. Stop fieldwork when new interviews **stop surprising** you.

## Exit gate for 1.1

**Proceed to next phase when ALL of:**

| Criterion | Evidence bar |
|-----------|--------------|
| **A1–A3 validated** (end users) | Recurring strain described **unprompted**; clear costs; past action (spend, time, workarounds) |
| **A4–A5 partial validation** (employers) | At least **a few** employers with **named, costed pain**, a **budget owner**, and **real pilot interest** |
| **A6 plausible** (facilitators) | Supply signal that culturally matched facilitators exist at **viable rates** — full economics can wait |
| **A7 supported** (all segments) | Consistent story of a **middle space** between "I'm fine" and psychiatry |

**Do not proceed if:**

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Pain does **not** recur unprompted | **Pause** — reframe the problem |
| Demand validates but **no buyer/budget** | **Iterate the model** — do not start building |
| A2 or A4 consistently weak | Revisit positioning, segment, or channel before build |

Record the exit decision and rationale at the bottom of `assumption-scorecard.md`.
